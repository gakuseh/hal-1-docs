Detailed Description

The filters applied to each sensor frame.

updateDataTask() calls apply() under g_ctrls_sensor_mutex, after Sensors::Update() has published a frame.

void SensorFilters::apply ( )

Filters g_telemNow's altitude, acceleration and rates in place and copies them into g_SensorData.

Rates are converted from deg/s to rad/s for g_SensorData. The caller must hold g_ctrls_sensor_mutex.
