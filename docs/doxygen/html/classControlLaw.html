Detailed Description

Gain-scheduled proportional roll-rate damper for the canards.

The command is u = −K · s · ω3, where ω3 is the estimated roll rate, s is the roll-effectiveness sign (±1), and K = I3 · λ / (∂M/∂ζ) places the closed-loop roll pole at −λ (RocketConfig::roll_damping_lambda). The output is rate limited, clamped, and forced to zero before burnout when RocketConfig::irec_compliant is set.

void ControlLaw::updateRollEffectivenessSign ( float t,
float w3_meas,
float u_prev_rad,
float vmag )

Roll effectiveness monitor: flips the gain sign if the canards act backwards.

Compares the measured roll acceleration (from successive gyro readings) with what the canard model predicts for the previous command. After rem_required_mismatches consecutive disagreements it flips the sign that computeControl() uses. Skipped until rem_post_burn_delay_s after burnout, for small commands, and for small accelerations. Does nothing unless RocketConfig::rem_enabled. Call once per loop.

Parameters
tFlight time (s).
w3_measCurrent measured roll rate (rad/s).
u_prev_radCommand applied in the previous step (rad).
vmagCurrent air-relative speed (m/s).
