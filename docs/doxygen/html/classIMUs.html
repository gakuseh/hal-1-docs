Detailed Description

Reads three BMI323 IMUs and votes them into one acceleration and rate.

In TMR mode, which Sensors uses, Update() reads all three sensors and TMR::Vote() averages the two that agree best. Otherwise sensor A is used alone. The ISM6HG256X high-g IMU is a member, but its init and update calls are commented out.
