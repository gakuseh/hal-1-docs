Detailed Description

6-DOF rocket model: time-varying mass properties, aerodynamics, and equations of motion.

Mass, inertia and CG vary linearly from ignition to burnout and are constant after. Atmosphere and wind come from a built-in weather profile. Forces and moments follow FV-Controls momentsforces.py.

◆ eom()

StateVec Physics::eom ( float t,
const StateVec & x,
float u_zeta,
float alt = 0.0f ) const

Full nonlinear equations of motion, ẋ = f(t, x, u).

Forces: gravity, thrust along +z, drag, body normal force, and canard normal force in the canard plane. Moments: corrective (base and canard), pitch/yaw damping, fin-cant roll forcing, roll damping, and the canard roll moment. Angle of attack is clamped to ±15°. Before t_rail there is no lift or moment. Rotation follows Euler's equations with I1 = I2.

Parameters
tFlight time (s).
xState.
u_zetaCanard deflection (rad).
altHeight above the launch site (m) for the weather lookup.
Returns
ẋ.

Measurement Jacobian ∂y/∂x (6×10).

Accelerometer rows use the velocity rows of A plus the derivatives of ω×v and of gravity in the body frame, scaled to g, with the angular-rate columns zeroed. Gyroscope rows are the identity on the rate columns.
