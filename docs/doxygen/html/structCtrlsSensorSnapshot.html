Detailed Description

One sensor sample handed from the sensor pipeline to the CTRLs task.

Sensors::Update() writes it, and SensorFilters::apply() then overwrites the altitude, acceleration and rates with filtered values, both under g_ctrls_sensor_mutex. CTRLs_STATESPACE() copies it and clears fresh. Body axes: x and y lateral, z along the rocket, positive forward.
